How I Read the Brief

  • The public site is the shell. Every portal feature moves into it and uses the AAC design system. The portal's data model and server rules stay the same.
  • Events have one source: the portal's events table. Published events fill the calendar. Completed events reach Past Events only after an officer publishes a recap.
  • Signing in with UCI Google adds My AAC to the header. Officers also get Officer Tools.
  • Sample data is Fall 2026. Reimbursement screens show the end of the quarter.
