02 / From invitation to expiry

Access with an ending

Grant access to an approved UCI or non-UCI member. Choose a future Pacific expiry, update their permissions, renew an expired grant, or revoke it.

Watch access end while a page is open

In any Guest preview, use “Expire in 10s” in the green demo bar, then open a dialog. The app closes privileged content and returns to My AAC. “Reset preview” restores sample access; reopen a permission link to return to its tools.

04 / Deliberate limits

Full officers keep the keys

Guest grants are club-wide. They do not make a member a full officer and do not apply to just one event.

Access & administration

Full officers retain officer grants, Guest grants, bans, audit history, application-form settings, and Google Sheets tools. Editing a board profile does not grant site access.

Financial boundaries

Guests with Reimbursements can manage routine drivers, trips, mileage, and payment records. Budget changes, quarter finalization, and archival remain full-officer actions.

Expiry & private data

The implementation checks permissions and expiry on protected API requests. Private ban reasons are withheld from Guests; payout details require Reimbursements access. Losing membership approval suspends Guest access.
